# Brand Guidelines & Business Rules: Dulces El Líder

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Core Concept:** "The King of Sweets." Combining traditional baking excellence with a modern, accessible leadership in the market.
- **Brand Promise:** Premium quality cakes and sweets at the best market price. We aim to offer products that look and taste like luxury but remain significantly more affordable than the competition.
- **Tone & Voice:** Traditional yet bold, reliable, and celebratory. We are "The Leader" (El Líder) because we don't compromise on quality for price.

## 2. Visual Identity & Assets
- **Primary Brand Color (Deep Burgundy):** `#600000`. This rich, deep red stimulates appetite and represents the passion and tradition behind every recipe. It provides a premium, "elite" backdrop for product presentation.
- **Secondary Brand Color (Refined Gold):** `#C5A059`. Used for the logo, borders, and high-value accents. It symbolizes the "Crown" of quality and the excellence of our ingredients.
- **Logo Symbolism:**
    - **The Crowned Cake:** Represents our status as the "Leader" in quality.
    - **The Circular Seal:** Acts as a "Stamp of Approval," signifying traditional craftsmanship and guaranteed freshness.
- **Typography Suggestion:**
    - **Script (for "Dulces"):** Evokes the handmade, artisanal nature of the products.
    - **Bold Serif/Sans (for "EL LÍDER"):** Projects strength, authority, and modern efficiency.

## 3. Pricing & Value Strategy
- **Positioning:** "Best Quality-Price Ratio." Every visual asset should reinforce the idea of a high-end product that is surprisingly accessible.
- **Catalog Presentation:** Use clean, high-contrast backgrounds (Deep Burgundy or Cream) to make the gold accents and the product itself "pop."

## 4. Operational Guidelines (n8n & Catalog)
- **Image Processing:** Products should be showcased with a professional border or "seal" inspired by the circular logo.
- **Branding:** The "El Líder" logo should be used as a watermark of quality on all processed catalog images.
- **Pricing Display:** Follow the dual-pricing rule (USD/CUP) if applicable, using the Refined Gold color for the price text to maintain the premium aesthetic.
